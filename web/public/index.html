The first thirty seconds

Your computer will warn you. Here's how to get past it.

Blurt isn't signed yet. Apple charges $99 a year for that and Microsoft rather more, and we'd rather keep Blurt free. So the first time you open it, your computer will say it can't verify who made it. It only happens once.

On a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Blurt into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click Blurt. macOS will refuse, saying it can't check it for malicious software. That's expected — click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Blurt. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  4. Tally will ask for the microphone and Accessibility. Both are needed: one to hear you, one to type for you.

On macOS 13 or 14 it's quicker: Control-click Blurt in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Apple removed that shortcut in macOS 15.

On Windows

  1. Run the .exe. Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” box.
  2. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer takes a few seconds and doesn't ask for admin rights.
  3. If your antivirus asks too, choose to keep it. It's flagging the missing signature, not anything it found in the app.
  4. Blurt lands in your tray, next to the clock. No permission prompts on Windows.

Nothing else to install. WebView2 is already on Windows 11, and the installer adds it on Windows 10.

Questions

Good to know

Is Blurt really free?

Yes. Dictation runs on your computer, so it costs nothing to run and there's nothing to subscribe to. Ask AI is free with Apple Intelligence or Ollama. If you choose Claude or OpenAI instead, you pay them directly with your own key.

Why does my computer say Blurt isn’t safe?

Because it isn’t signed. Apple and Microsoft charge a yearly fee to vouch for an app, and Blurt is free, so we haven’t paid it yet. Your computer can’t tell who made the app, so it warns you once. Opening it the first time takes one extra click, and Blurt is signed properly the moment we can justify the fee.

Which key do I hold?

Right ⌥ Option on Mac and Right Ctrl on Windows, plus Shift to ask AI. You can pick a different key in Settings. Press Esc while holding to cancel.

Does it work in every app?

Anywhere you can type: chat apps, email, docs, code editors, browsers. Blurt pastes the text for you, then puts back whatever you had copied.

What do I need?

An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 13 or later, or a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, plus about 1 GB of free space. The requirements have the full picture. Intel Macs aren’t supported.

Which languages does it understand?

English plus 24 European languages, including Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and Polish.

Why does macOS ask for Accessibility access?

So Blurt can notice when you hold the talk key, read the text you've highlighted, and paste into the app you're using. Blurt doesn't record your screen or keystrokes.
